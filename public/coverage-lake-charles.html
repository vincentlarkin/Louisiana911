Lake Charles police calls

The Lake Charles Police Department publishes its closed calls through its Police-to-Citizen site about 24 hours after they close. Louisiana911 checks that list every 30 minutes and shows the calls on the map and in the list.

  • Source: LCPD closed calls on Police-to-Citizen
  • Timing: Delayed about 24 hours
  • Includes: traffic, crime-related, and service calls, plus officer activity

Latest shows up to 150 calls from the past seven days. Every imported call stays in History under its original date. LCPD doesn't publish unit counts.

Map locations

LCPD generalizes its locations, so markers are approximate. Only the source's own map points are used; a call without one stays in the list without a marker.

Limits

These are LCPD calls only, not every agency in Calcasieu Parish. A closed call isn't a finding that a crime happened, and nothing here is live.
