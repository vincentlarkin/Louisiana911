Departments and incident information

The public feed can identify Shreveport Police, Shreveport Fire, the Caddo Parish Sheriff's Office, Caddo Fire Districts, and publicly listed EMS activity. Louisiana911 preserves the published incident description, reported time, street, cross streets, municipality, responding-agency code, and unit count.

  • SPD: Shreveport Police Department incidents listed by the source.
  • SFD: Shreveport Fire Department incidents listed by the source.
  • CSO: Caddo Parish Sheriff's Office incidents listed by the source.
  • CFD: Caddo Parish fire-district activity listed by the source.

How Caddo locations reach the map

Louisiana911 checks addresses and intersections against source-aware Caddo Parish bounds. When the feed supplies a named street between two cross streets, the map can use the validated midpoint of that street segment. Provider results that only return a broad street-name centroid are rejected rather than shown as a plausible-looking false point.

Active does not mean unresolved

The official page is an active-event list, not a complete case record. Incidents can change or disappear when the source updates. A listed call is not proof that a crime occurred, and removal does not establish a particular disposition.
