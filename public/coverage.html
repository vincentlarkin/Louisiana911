How the map handles locations

Louisiana911 keeps each source's incident label, time, agency, and location as published. Approximate locations are marked, and a location that can't be matched is left off the map rather than placed at a city center.

Independent and unofficial. Louisiana911 is not an emergency service and isn't affiliated with any government, 911 district, or responding agency. In an emergency, call 911.
