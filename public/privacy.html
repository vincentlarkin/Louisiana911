Privacy

This covers the louisiana911.com website and the Louisiana 911 Android app. There are no accounts, no ads, and nothing is sold.

Last updated November 14, 2026.

Your location

"Near me" on the website and in the app uses your device's location to sort calls by distance. That happens on your device. Your location is not sent to Louisiana911 or stored anywhere. You can say no and pick a city instead.

The Android app

  • The app has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • It downloads the call list and map from louisiana911.com. Those requests carry the app's name and version, plus a short-lived access token that identifies the app but not you.
  • Sharing a call uses Android's share menu; the app doesn't see where you send it.

The website

  • Analytics: the website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which features people use: pages viewed, clicks, scrolling, load speed, device type and rough location from your IP address. Google sets cookies for this. You can block it with any content blocker and the site still works.
  • History cookie: a cookie named l911_history_ui lets the History tab load. It holds no personal information and expires within a day.
  • Settings: things like your last tab and map style are kept in your browser's local storage, not on our server.
  • Address search: when you search an address in Reports, the address is sent to our server and on to Esri's ArcGIS service to find it on the map.

Server logs

Like any website, our server sees your IP address and browser or app version with each request. We use them to stop abuse and limit how fast one visitor can download history. Rate-limit records keep a scrambled form of the IP address for at most a day. Requests pass through Cloudflare, which provides the site's connection and protection.

Children

Louisiana911 isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
