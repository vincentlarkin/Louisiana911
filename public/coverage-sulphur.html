Sulphur police calls

The Sulphur Police Department publishes its closed calls through its Police-to-Citizen site about 24 hours after they close. Louisiana911 checks that list every 30 minutes and shows the calls on the map and in the list.

  • Source: Sulphur PD closed calls on Police-to-Citizen
  • Timing: Delayed about 24 hours
  • Includes: traffic stops and complaints, crashes, alarms, suspicious activity, and service calls

Latest shows up to 150 calls from the past seven days. Every imported call stays in History under its original date. Sulphur PD doesn't publish unit counts.

Map locations

Sulphur PD generalizes its locations to the block, so markers are approximate. Only the source's own map points are used; a call without one stays in the list without a marker.

Limits

These are Sulphur PD calls only. Some are in Westlake or nearby parts of Calcasieu Parish, but this isn't every agency in the area. A closed call isn't a finding that a crime happened, and nothing here is live.
